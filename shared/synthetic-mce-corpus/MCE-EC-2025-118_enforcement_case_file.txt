------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALLEGED BREACH
------------------------------------------------------------
During a site inspection on 2025-08-14, inspectors found six VSAT
terminals operating at the Warri Industrial Park site that were not
registered with the licensing register, contrary to Licence
Condition 4.5 (Terminal Registration).

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CASE TIMELINE
------------------------------------------------------------
2025-08-14   Inspection carried out. Case opened.
2025-08-28   Notice of Violation issued to the respondent,
             requiring a written response within 14 days.
2025-09-19   Written response received from the respondent (after
             the 14-day period). The respondent admitted the
             terminals were unregistered and cited an internal
             administrative error.
2025-11-05   Enforcement hearing held. The respondent was
             represented by its Regulatory Affairs Manager.
2025-11-26   Decision issued (see section 3).
2026-01-20   Respondent reported that all six terminals had been
             registered. Verified by the case officer.
2026-02-02   Case closed as compliant.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------
On 2025-11-26 the enforcement panel decided that:

(a) the respondent pays an administrative penalty of NGN
    5,000,000; and

(b) the respondent registers all unregistered terminals within 60
    days of the date of the decision, that is, on or before
    2026-01-25.
