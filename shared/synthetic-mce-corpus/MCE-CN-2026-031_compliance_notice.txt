MONITORING, COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT (MCE)
NOTICE OF NON-COMPLIANCE

Notice reference:       MCE-CN-2026-031
Date of notice:         2026-03-09
Issued by:              Head, Compliance Monitoring
Addressed to:           The Managing Director
                        Sahel Link Networks Ltd
Licence number:         VSAT-OP-0192
Licence category:       VSAT Service Operator (Class B)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. NATURE OF NON-COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------
Our records show that Sahel Link Networks Ltd has not submitted its
Quarterly Service Quality Report for the fourth quarter of 2025
(1 October 2025 to 31 December 2025). The report was due on 30
January 2026.

This is a breach of Licence Condition 7.2 (Quarterly Reporting),
which requires every VSAT Service Operator to submit a Quarterly
Service Quality Report within 30 days after the end of each quarter.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. REQUIRED ACTION
------------------------------------------------------------
Sahel Link Networks Ltd is required to:

(a) submit the complete Quarterly Service Quality Report for Q4
    2025 to the Compliance Monitoring Unit within 21 days of the
    date of this notice, that is, on or before 2026-03-30; and

(b) confirm in writing the name of the officer responsible for
    future quarterly submissions.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. REPRESENTATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
The operator may submit written representations explaining the
failure within 7 days of the date of this notice. Representations
do not extend the deadline in section 2 unless the Compliance
Monitoring Unit confirms an extension in writing.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONSEQUENCES OF CONTINUED DEFAULT
------------------------------------------------------------
If the report is not received by the deadline, an administrative
penalty of NGN 2,500,000 will apply for each full week of continued
default, up to a maximum of NGN 10,000,000. Repeated or prolonged
default may be referred for enforcement action under the MCE
enforcement procedure.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. CONTACT
------------------------------------------------------------
Queries on this notice should be addressed to the Compliance
Monitoring Unit, quoting reference MCE-CN-2026-031.

------------------------------------------------------------
REVISION NOTICE (issued 2026-03-20)
------------------------------------------------------------
Following a written request from Sahel Link Networks Ltd dated
2026-03-16, the Compliance Monitoring Unit has agreed to extend the
deadline in section 2(a) to 2026-04-13. All other requirements of
this notice remain unchanged. This revision supersedes the deadline
stated in section 2(a).
