Quick question

“Driver, fuel go reach Ojota?” 🚐

🧑🏾‍✈️

The danfo driver's sum

He doesn't guess. He looks at the gauge, remembers what one trip burns, and divides.

litres_left   = 20
litres_a_trip = 4
trips_left    = 20 / 4   # 5 trips
⛽

That is remaining useful life

How much is left above the limit, divided by how fast it is going.

And the smart driver fills up before the light comes on, not at Ojota under the bridge. 😅

The whole method is two small functions

fit_trend + day_of_threshold

x = np.arange(len(series))                  # day number 0, 1, 2, ...
slope, intercept = np.polyfit(x, series.values, 1)   # the best straight line

line = np.poly1d([slope, intercept])
future = line(np.arange(len(series) + 4000))         # extend it 4,000 days
crossed = future < threshold                         # True where it is below the limit
day = np.argmax(crossed)                             # the FIRST True

np.polyfit(x, y, 1) gives slope and intercept: two numbers for a straight line.

np.argmax of True/False returns the position of the first True. Remember that. 👀

The bug · Week 6 code book, topic 6.4

np.argmax of “never” is 0 🐞

crossed = [False, False, False, False]
np.argmax(crossed)        # 0 !!!

# FIXED in day_of_threshold:
if not crossed.any():
    return None           # "no crossing"

The line never reaches 5 kg inside the 4,000-day search (it stops on 13 Dec 2034). Every value is False, and argmax quietly says 0.

Honest answer: “no crossing before 13 Dec 2034”. Then look further.

Why does the straight line call it early?

The fade is slowing down 🐢

A line fitted on the early, faster years overstates the later fade, so it reaches 80% too soon.
Early is the safe side for a battery. But you only know which way you err because you backtested.

Look closer at the battery

The battery ages when it works 🌘

In eclipse season (27 Feb – 13 Apr, 1 Sep – 16 Oct) the battery carries the satellite through the shadow every night,
and loses capacity 2.3× faster per day. Like your inverter battery in a month of heavy NEPA cuts. 🔌

Five golden rules for remaining useful life

How a professional says it 🧑🏾‍🔧

1

Report a window, never a single day

2

Say how far past the data you reach

3

Act before the crossing

4

Re-estimate regularly

5

“No crossing” is an answer, not day 0

🔋 Battery: first dips below 80% in mid-May 2025, below for good by late July. Act before May.

📡 Amplifier: around 2033 (calls span Mar–Nov), 7+ years past our data. Re-check every year.

⛽ Propellant: late 2035 to early 2036 if the burn plan holds.
