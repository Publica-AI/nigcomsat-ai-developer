The recipe

Three lines, three parts

from statsmodels.tsa.seasonal import seasonal_decompose
parts = seasonal_decompose(kam["availability_pct"],
                           model="additive",    # or "multiplicative"
                           period=12)           # one rainy season = 12 months
parts.trend      # the slow direction
parts.seasonal   # the repeating pattern
parts.resid      # the leftovers

⚠️ No gaps allowed

NaN inside the series → ValueError. Fill or trim first.

⚠️ Two full cycles minimum

period=12 needs at least 24 rows, or it refuses to run.

Add or multiply? ➕✖️

Look at the swing. Does it stay the same size, or grow?

additive_multiplicative_demo.csv

Same trend, same season… different maths

➕ Additive

Yearly swing: 0.29 → 0.28. Stays the same size.

🚌 Like December fare going up ₦100 every year: a fixed amount.

✖️ Multiplicative

Yearly swing: 0.29 → 0.54. Grows with the level.

🚌 Like December fare going up 20%: 20% of a bigger fare is more naira.

A season that comes and goes needs a smarter tool

😵 classic seasonal_decompose

Uses the same daily pattern for all 120 days, so the eclipse hours flood the residual. 46 of the top 46 leftovers are just eclipse.

🙌 STL(robust=True)

Lets the season change slowly. The injected array dropouts rank #4–#7; after them nothing is bigger than 1.75 A.
