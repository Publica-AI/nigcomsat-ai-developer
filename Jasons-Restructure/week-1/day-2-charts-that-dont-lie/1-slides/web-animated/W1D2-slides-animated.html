Where should the y-axis start?

It depends on what you measure

📈 Percentages near the top

South South uptime

0–100: flat, useless

96–99: story visible ✅

Zoom in, and write the range on the chart.

🎫 Counts (tickets)

average NOC tickets per weekday

axis from 3 ❌ Sunday looks dead

axis from 0 ✅ honest

Things you can add up: start at zero.

The 4-sentence finding

How to report it to your unit head

1

Situation: The Ka-band Nigeria beam carries customer traffic through the rainy season.

2

Observation: In August the link was down for 12 hours across 11 days, yet the daily average never fell below 4.91 dB.

3

Implication: Reports built on daily averages would show zero outages and miss every SLA breach.

4

Recommendation: Report availability from hourly minimums, with the 0 dB line on every chart.
