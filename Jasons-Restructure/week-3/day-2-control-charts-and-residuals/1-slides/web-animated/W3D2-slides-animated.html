Anatomy

Three lines, all learned from a quiet stretch

➖

Centre line

the mean of a known-good reference period

⬆️

UCL

upper control limit = centre + 3σ

⬇️

LCL

lower control limit = centre − 3σ

❌ Not the min and max ever seen

One bad day would stretch them forever.

❌ Not the engineering design limits

Those say “broken”. Control limits say “unusual”.

Why 3σ and not 2σ?

Every alarm costs attention

On perfectly normal data, how many hours land outside the limits by pure chance in 2,880 hours (120 days)?

±2σ → 4.6% of hours → ≈ 131 false alarms 😩

±3σ → 0.27% of hours → ≈ 8 false alarms 😌

On our bus data with rolling limits: ±2σ fires 31 times, ±3σ fires 5.

Operating states

One satellite, three moods 🌗

In eclipse the array gives ~0 A. Just after eclipse it works extra to recharge the battery.

Rule: separate limits for each state

Mixed, σ grows from 0.073 to 0.594 A and nothing fires. Don't delete hours or drop to 2σ.

Why median and MAD?

MAD × 1.4826 = same size as σ for normal data, so “3 robust σ” means what you expect.

Common cause vs special cause

Don't touch normal wobble 🙅🏾

Common cause: the everyday wobble inside the band. Leave it.

Special cause: outside the band, or a strange pattern. Investigate.

Tampering = reacting to common-cause wobble as if it were special. It makes things worse.

What you now know

📏

Control chart: centre ± 3σ from a quiet reference, one set of limits per operating state, refreshed so it doesn't go stale.

🛡️

Robust: median + MAD × 1.4826, so one wild hour can't move “normal”. Don't tamper with common-cause wobble.

🔍

Residual = observed − expected. Check it's clean. Great for spikes, weak on shifts, useless on slow drift.
