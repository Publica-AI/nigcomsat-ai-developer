Step 2 · the CUSUM idea

Keep a running total of small leftovers 🧾

S = max(0, S + leftover − k)        # every day
➕

Add today's leftover

a bit hot? S grows a bit

🤏

Minus the slack k

ordinary noise can't pile up

🚨

S above h → alarm

h = how much evidence you need

max(0, …) means S never goes below zero: good days can't “bank” credit. 🏦❌

The two knobs

k and h: set them from σ

🤏

k = 0.5 × σ: the slack

Ignore wobble smaller than half a σ. Ordinary noise does not accumulate.

Our amplifier: σ = 0.20 °C → k = 0.10 °C

🚨

h = 5 × σ: the decision limit

How much evidence before you shout. Low h = fast but noisy. High h = calm but late.

Our amplifier: h = 1.02 °C
