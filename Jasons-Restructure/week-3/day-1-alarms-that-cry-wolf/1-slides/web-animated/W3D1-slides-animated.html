What alarm software already does

Four tricks your NOC already uses

🟡🔴

Yellow & red limits

Warning first, then alarm. Like the fuel light, then the car stopping.

⏳

Persistence

Only alarm if it stays bad for N readings. “Let me see if NEPA really took light.”

↕️

Hysteresis

Alarm ON at one level, OFF at another, so it doesn't flap on/off all night.

🎭

State-based limits

Different “normal” for different modes: eclipse, recharge, sunlit.

Each trick buys quiet… and each one can make you blind. Today we test them on data.

But what IS normal?

The faults you hunt widen your own limits

📦 All sunlit hours

σ = 0.59

Includes the dropouts and the post-eclipse recharge boost. Band 26.4–30.0 A: wide, quiet.

🧼 “Clean” hours only

σ = 0.17

Remove every odd hour. Band 27.65–28.66 A: 143 alarms, 138 of them normal recharge hours. 🐺

Normal needs context (sunlit? recharging? eclipse?), not just a mean and a σ. Day 2 builds this properly.
