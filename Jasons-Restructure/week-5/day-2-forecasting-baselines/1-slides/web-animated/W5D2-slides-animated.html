Weeks 3–5: detection

Weeks 5–6: forecasting

Guess a reading that hasn't happened yet. How many tickets next week? When will the battery reach its limit?

A forecast is a guess about the future made only from the past.
If your forecast secretly used next week's number, it's not a forecast. It's expo. 🙅🏾
