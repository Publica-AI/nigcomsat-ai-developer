------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure describes the steps for onboarding a new employee,
from offer acceptance to the end of the probation period.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PRE-ARRIVAL (HR, 5 working days before start date)
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Send the welcome email with the employee's start date,
    reporting time (09:00), office location (14th Floor, Civic
    Towers, Ozumba Mbadiwe Avenue, Victoria Island, Lagos),
    dress code (business casual) and first-day contact person.

(b) Create the employee's record in the HRIS with name, role,
    department, grade level, line manager, and start date.

(c) Notify the IT department to provision the following:
    - Company laptop (configured with standard software)
    - Microsoft 365 account (email, Teams, SharePoint)
    - Slack account
    - VPN licence
    - Access to department-specific systems (as requested by
      the line manager)

(d) Notify Facilities to assign a desk, access card and
    parking (if applicable).

(e) Prepare the onboarding pack: employment contract (two
    copies for signing), NDA, company handbook, org chart,
    benefits summary.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. DAY ONE
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The first-day contact person meets the new employee at
    reception and escorts them to their desk.

(b) HR conducts a 1-hour orientation session covering:
    - Company history and structure
    - HR policies: leave (HR-POL-2025-006), code of conduct
      (HR-POL-2024-002), remote work (HR-POL-2024-003),
      data protection (HR-POL-2025-004)
    - Benefits: HMO enrolment, pension (PFA selection),
      group life insurance
    - Payroll: salary date (25th of each month), bank account
      form, TIN confirmation

(c) IT conducts a 30-minute setup session:
    - Laptop handover and login
    - VPN setup and test
    - Slack channels to join: #general, #announcements, and
      the department channel
    - Password policy: 12+ characters, changed every 90 days,
      MFA enabled on all accounts

(d) The line manager introduces the team, assigns a buddy
    (a peer in the same department), and shares the
    30-60-90 day plan.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. FIRST WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The buddy checks in daily for the first week.

(b) The employee completes mandatory training modules on the
    learning portal:
    - Information Security Awareness (1 hour)
    - NDPA and Data Protection (45 minutes)
    - Workplace Harassment Prevention (30 minutes)

(c) The line manager schedules a 30-minute end-of-week check-in
    to review progress against the 30-day goals.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROBATION PERIOD
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The standard probation period is 6 months.

(b) The line manager conducts a formal review at the end of
    month 3 and month 6, using the Probation Review Form on
    the HRIS.

(c) At the end of month 6, the line manager recommends one of:
    - Confirmation — the employee becomes a confirmed staff
      member with full benefits and leave entitlement
    - Extension — probation extended by up to 3 months, with
      documented improvement goals
    - Termination — with 2 weeks' notice and documented reasons

(d) HR issues the confirmation letter within 5 working days of
    the line manager's recommendation.
