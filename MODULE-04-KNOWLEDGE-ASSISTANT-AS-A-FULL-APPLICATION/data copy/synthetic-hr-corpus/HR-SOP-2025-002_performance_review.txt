1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure describes the annual performance review cycle,
the rating scale, and how review outcomes connect to promotions,
bonuses and development plans.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. REVIEW CYCLE
------------------------------------------------------------
The performance year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

(a) January — Goal setting
    Each employee and their line manager agree on 3 to 5
    SMART objectives for the year, recorded in the HRIS.
    At least one objective must relate to professional
    development.

(b) July — Mid-year check-in
    A 30-minute conversation between the employee and line
    manager. No formal rating. The purpose is to review
    progress, adjust objectives if business needs have
    changed, and flag any support required.

(c) December — Year-end review
    The employee completes a self-assessment. The line
    manager completes the manager assessment. Both are
    submitted in the HRIS by 15 December.

(d) January (following year) — Calibration and feedback
    The Head of Department reviews all ratings in a
    calibration meeting with HR to ensure consistency. Final
    ratings are communicated to employees by 31 January.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. RATING SCALE
------------------------------------------------------------
| Rating | Label               | Description |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| 5      | Exceptional         | Consistently exceeded all objectives; impact beyond own role |
| 4      | Exceeds expectations| Met all and exceeded some objectives |
| 3      | Meets expectations  | Met all core objectives |
| 2      | Needs improvement   | Met some but not all objectives; gaps identified |
| 1      | Unsatisfactory      | Failed to meet most objectives; performance plan required |

A rating of 1 or 2 triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP),
documented in the HRIS, with a 90-day review period.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. LINK TO COMPENSATION
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Annual bonus (discretionary, subject to company performance):
    - Rating 5: up to 20% of annual base salary
    - Rating 4: up to 15%
    - Rating 3: up to 10%
    - Rating 2 or 1: not eligible

(b) Promotion eligibility:
    - Minimum rating of 4 in the current year and 3 in the
      prior year.
    - At least 18 months in the current grade level.
    - Recommendation from the line manager and Head of
      Department.
    - Promotions are approved by the Managing Director in a
      quarterly promotion committee meeting (March, June,
      September, December).

(c) Salary review:
    - Annual salary review is effective 1 April. The percentage
      increase is linked to the performance rating and market
      benchmarking conducted by HR.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. DISPUTES
------------------------------------------------------------
If an employee disagrees with their rating, they may submit a
written appeal to the Head of Department within 10 working days
of receiving the final rating. The Head of Department and the
Director of People & Culture will review and respond within 15
working days. Their decision is final.
