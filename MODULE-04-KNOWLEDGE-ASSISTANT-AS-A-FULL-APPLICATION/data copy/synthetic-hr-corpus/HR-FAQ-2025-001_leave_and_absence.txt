Q1: How many days of annual leave do I get?
A1: Confirmed full-time employees get 25 working days per year.
    This was increased from 20 days in July 2025 (see circular
    HR-CIR-2025-001).

Q2: I joined in September. Do I get the full 25 days?
A2: No. Your entitlement is pro-rated from your confirmation
    date. If you are still on probation, you do not accrue
    annual leave — probation is 6 months from your start date.

Q3: Can I carry over unused leave?
A3: Yes, up to 10 days. They must be used by 31 March of the
    following year. After that, they are forfeited.

Q4: Can I get paid for unused leave instead of taking it?
A4: Not while you are employed. Leave cash-out is only
    available on termination of employment.

Q5: How much sick leave do I have?
A5: 12 working days per year. You need a medical certificate
    if you are away for 3 or more consecutive days.

Q6: What happens if I use all my sick leave?
A6: Additional sick leave beyond 12 days is treated as leave
    without pay unless your condition qualifies for extended
    coverage under the company HMO.

Q7: How long is maternity leave?
A7: 16 weeks — 4 weeks before the expected delivery date and
    12 weeks after. Fully paid.

Q8: How long is paternity leave?
A8: 10 working days, taken within 3 months of the child's
    birth. Fully paid.

Q9: My mother passed away. What leave am I entitled to?
A9: 5 working days of compassionate leave for the death of an
    immediate family member (parent, spouse, child, sibling).
    This does not come out of your annual leave.

Q10: My uncle passed away. Does compassionate leave apply?
A10: Yes, 3 working days for extended family (grandparent,
     in-law, uncle, aunt). Also not deducted from annual leave.

Q11: I need to take time off but I have no leave left. What
     are my options?
A11: You can apply for leave without pay, up to 15 working
     days per year. This requires approval from your Head of
     Department and the Director, People & Culture.

Q12: Do public holidays count as part of my annual leave?
A12: No. Public holidays are separate. See the annual Public
     Holiday Schedule circular (HR-CIR-2025-002) for dates.

Q13: I want to take leave during the December closure. Do I
     have to use annual leave?
A13: The office closure uses 3 working days of annual leave
     (the working days between Christmas and New Year that are
     not public holidays). If you prefer to work remotely
     during this period, discuss with your line manager — you
     keep those 3 days.
