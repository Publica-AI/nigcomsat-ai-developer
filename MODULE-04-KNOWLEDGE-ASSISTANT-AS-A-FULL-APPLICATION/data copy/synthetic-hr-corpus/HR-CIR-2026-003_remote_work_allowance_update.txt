Dear Colleagues,

Effective 1 March 2026, the following changes apply to the
remote work equipment allowance described in the Remote Work
Policy (HR-POL-2024-003):

1. MONTHLY ALLOWANCE INCREASE
   The monthly Remote Work Allowance increases from NGN 25,000
   to NGN 35,000, reflecting higher electricity and internet
   costs. This applies to all employees on an approved hybrid
   or fully remote arrangement.

2. EQUIPMENT BUDGET
   The one-time equipment request limit (for an ergonomic chair,
   monitor, or similar) increases from NGN 80,000 to NGN 120,000.
   Requests up to NGN 120,000 are approved by the IT Manager;
   requests above that amount require Head of Department approval.

3. CO-WORKING SPACE REIMBURSEMENT
   Employees on a fully remote arrangement who live outside Lagos
   may now claim up to NGN 40,000 per month for a co-working
   space, subject to Head of Department pre-approval and original
   receipts. This is a new benefit not previously available.

4. HOW TO CLAIM
   - The increased monthly allowance will be reflected
     automatically in your March salary.
   - For equipment requests, use the IT portal as before.
   - For co-working space claims, submit receipts through the
     Expense Claim Form (see HR-SOP-2025-004).

NOTE: The Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-2024-003) itself is not
being reissued at this time. This circular updates only the
allowance amounts in section 4 of that policy. All other terms
— eligibility, working hours, data protection obligations —
remain unchanged.

If you have questions, contact HR Operations at
