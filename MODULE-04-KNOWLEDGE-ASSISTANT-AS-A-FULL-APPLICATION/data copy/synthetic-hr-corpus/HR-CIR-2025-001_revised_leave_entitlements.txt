Dear Colleagues,

Following a review of our leave benefits, Management has approved
the following changes, effective 1 July 2025:

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
   Your annual leave entitlement increases from 20 to 25 working
   days per calendar year. The updated policy is HR-POL-2025-006.

2. CARRY-FORWARD
   You may now carry forward up to 10 unused leave days (previously
   5) to the following year. Carried-forward days must be used by
   31 March, or they are forfeited.

3. COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
   Compassionate leave is now a formal entitlement:
   - 5 days for the death of an immediate family member
   - 3 days for the death of an extended family member
   These days are in addition to annual leave.

4. SICK LEAVE
   Sick leave is formalised at 12 working days per year. A medical
   certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive
   days.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
   - Maternity leave: 16 weeks (4 weeks before, 12 weeks after
     delivery), fully paid.
   - Paternity leave: 10 working days, to be taken within 3
     months of the child's birth.

6. WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
   Nothing. The HR portal has been updated with the new entitlements
   for all confirmed employees. Your leave balance now reflects
   the 25-day annual entitlement, pro-rated for the remainder of
   2025.

   If you have questions, contact HR Operations at
   hr-ops@novanet.com.ng or extension 4200.

Thank you.
