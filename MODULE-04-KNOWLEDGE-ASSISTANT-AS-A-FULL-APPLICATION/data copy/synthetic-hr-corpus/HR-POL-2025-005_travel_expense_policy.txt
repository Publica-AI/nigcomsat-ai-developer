------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy governs business travel arrangements and the
reimbursement of expenses incurred by employees while travelling
on company business.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy applies to all employees travelling on behalf of
NovaNet Technologies Ltd, whether within Nigeria or
internationally.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. TRAVEL APPROVAL
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's
    line manager using the Travel Request Form on the HR portal.

(b) International travel requires additional approval from the
    Head of Department and the Director, Finance.

(c) Travel requests must be submitted at least 10 working days
    before the departure date (5 working days for domestic
    travel).

------------------------------------------------------------
4. TRAVEL ENTITLEMENTS — DOMESTIC
------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Transportation
    - Air travel: economy class for all grades. Bookings are
      made through the company's designated travel agent.
    - Road travel: company vehicle where available, or a
      reimbursement of NGN 80 per kilometre for personal vehicle
      use. Receipts and an odometer log are required.
    - Ride-hailing (Bolt, Uber): reimbursed for airport
      transfers and local travel during the trip, with receipts.

4.2 Accommodation
    - Grade Level 1–4: up to NGN 35,000 per night
    - Grade Level 5–7: up to NGN 55,000 per night
    - Grade Level 8 and above: up to NGN 85,000 per night
    - Bookings should be made through the travel agent. If the
      employee books independently, only the actual cost up to
      the limit is reimbursed.

4.3 Meals and incidentals
    - A per diem of NGN 15,000 per day covers meals, local
      transport within the destination city, and incidentals.
    - The per diem is paid in advance and does not require
      individual receipts.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRAVEL ENTITLEMENTS — INTERNATIONAL
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Air travel: economy class for Grade Level 1–7; premium
    economy for Grade Level 8 and above. Business class
    requires Managing Director approval.

(b) Accommodation: actual cost up to USD 200 per night (or
    equivalent in local currency). Exceptions for high-cost
    cities (London, New York, Dubai) may be approved by the
    Director, Finance.

(c) Per diem: USD 75 per day for meals and incidentals.

(d) Travel insurance is arranged by the company for all
    international trips.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXPENSE CLAIMS
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Claims must be submitted within 14 days of the employee's
    return, using the Expense Claim Form on the HR portal.

(b) All claims must be supported by original receipts or
    electronic equivalents. Claims without receipts are not
    reimbursed, except for the per diem.

(c) The Finance team processes approved claims within 10
    working days of receipt.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
------------------------------------------------------------
The following are not reimbursable:
- Alcoholic beverages
- Personal entertainment (cinema, spa, tourism)
- Traffic fines or parking tickets
- Upgrades to a higher travel class without approval
- Mini-bar charges
- Laundry for trips shorter than 5 days
