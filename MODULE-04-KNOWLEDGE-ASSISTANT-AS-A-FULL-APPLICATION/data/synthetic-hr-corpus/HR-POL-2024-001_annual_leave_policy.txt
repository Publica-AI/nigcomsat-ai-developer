1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy sets out the entitlements and procedures for annual
leave for all employees of NovaNet Technologies Ltd.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy applies to all confirmed full-time employees. Contract
staff and interns are covered by separate arrangements specified in
their engagement letters.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) All confirmed employees are entitled to 20 working days of
    paid annual leave per calendar year.

(b) Employees who join part-way through the year receive a
    pro-rated entitlement based on their start date.

(c) Leave entitlement may not be carried forward to the next
    calendar year without written approval from the employee's
    line manager and the HR department. A maximum of 5 days may
    be carried forward.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. REQUESTING LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal
    at least 7 working days before the intended start date.

(b) Leave of 5 or more consecutive days requires 14 working
    days' notice.

(c) The line manager must approve or decline the request within
    3 working days. If no response is given, the request is
    escalated to the Head of Department.

(d) Leave requests during project-critical periods (as declared
    by the Head of Department) may be deferred.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
------------------------------------------------------------
Public holidays declared by the Federal Government of Nigeria
are in addition to annual leave. A list of recognised public
holidays for each year is published by HR no later than December
of the preceding year (see the annual Public Holiday Schedule
circular).

------------------------------------------------------------
6. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY
------------------------------------------------------------
Where an employee has exhausted their annual leave and needs
additional time off, they may apply for leave without pay. This
requires approval from the Head of Department and the Director,
People & Culture. Leave without pay may not exceed 10 working
days in any calendar year.
