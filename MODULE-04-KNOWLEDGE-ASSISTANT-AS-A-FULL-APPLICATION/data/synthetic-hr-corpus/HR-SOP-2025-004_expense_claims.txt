1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure details how employees submit, document and track
expense claims for business-related costs not covered by
company-issued cards or direct billing.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
------------------------------------------------------------
Expenses eligible for reimbursement are those incurred directly
for company business and consistent with the Travel and Expense
Reimbursement Policy (HR-POL-2025-005). Common categories:

(a) Travel — flights, ground transport, fuel for personal
    vehicle on company business (NGN 80/km with odometer log)
(b) Accommodation — within the limits for the employee's grade
    level as specified in HR-POL-2025-005
(c) Meals — during business travel (covered by per diem) or
    client-facing meals with pre-approval
(d) Office supplies — items under NGN 10,000 purchased for
    immediate project needs when procurement lead time is
    impractical
(e) Professional development — conference registration, exam
    fees, or books pre-approved by the line manager

------------------------------------------------------------
3. SUBMISSION PROCESS
------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 — Collect receipts
    Original receipts or clear electronic copies (PDF, photo)
    for every item. Bank or card statements alone are not
    accepted. Per diem does not require receipts.

Step 2 — Complete the Expense Claim Form
    On the HR portal, enter: date of expense, category,
    description, amount (NGN), and attach the receipt. Group
    expenses by trip or project.

Step 3 — Line manager approval
    The line manager reviews and approves or returns the claim
    within 3 working days. Claims above NGN 100,000 also
    require Head of Department approval.

Step 4 — Finance processing
    The Finance team verifies receipts, checks policy
    compliance, and processes the payment within 10 working
    days of receiving the approved claim. Payment is made to
    the employee's salary bank account.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. DEADLINES
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Claims must be submitted within 14 days of the expense
    being incurred or the employee's return from travel,
    whichever is later.

(b) Claims submitted after 30 days require Head of Department
    approval and a written explanation for the delay.

(c) Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. REJECTED CLAIMS
------------------------------------------------------------
If Finance rejects a claim (missing receipt, policy violation,
insufficient documentation), the employee is notified via
email with the reason. The employee may resubmit with the
missing documentation within 7 working days.
