------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy replaces HR-POL-2024-001 and sets out the updated
entitlements and procedures for annual leave for all employees
of NovaNet Technologies Ltd.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy applies to all confirmed full-time employees. Contract
staff and interns are covered by separate arrangements specified in
their engagement letters.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) All confirmed employees are entitled to 25 working days of
    paid annual leave per calendar year.

(b) Employees in their first year of employment receive a
    pro-rated entitlement calculated from their confirmation
    date, not their start date.

(c) Unused leave may be carried forward to the next calendar
    year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Carried-forward days
    must be used by 31 March of the following year, or they
    are forfeited.

(d) Employees may not "cash out" unused leave days except on
    termination of employment, at which point unused days are
    paid at the employee's daily rate.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. REQUESTING LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal
    at least 10 working days before the intended start date.

(b) Leave of 5 or more consecutive days requires 15 working
    days' notice.

(c) The line manager must approve or decline the request within
    2 working days. If no response is given within 2 working
    days, the request is automatically escalated to the Head
    of Department.

(d) During the December holiday period (15 December to 5 January),
    leave requests are approved on a first-come, first-served
    basis. Each team must have at least 30% of its members
    available during this period.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
In addition to annual leave, employees are entitled to:

(a) 5 working days of compassionate leave on the death of an
    immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

(b) 3 working days of compassionate leave on the death of an
    extended family member (grandparent, in-law, uncle, aunt).

(c) Compassionate leave is not deducted from the annual leave
    entitlement.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. SICK LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Employees are entitled to 12 working days of paid sick
    leave per calendar year.

(b) Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a
    medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

(c) Sick leave beyond the 12-day entitlement is treated as
    leave without pay, unless the employee's medical condition
    qualifies for extended sick leave under the company's HMO
    arrangement.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Female employees are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity
    leave, taken as 4 weeks before and 12 weeks after the
    expected date of delivery.

(b) Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid
    paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's
    birth.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
------------------------------------------------------------
Public holidays declared by the Federal Government of Nigeria
are in addition to annual leave. See the annual Public Holiday
Schedule circular (HR-CIR series).

------------------------------------------------------------
9. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY
------------------------------------------------------------
Where an employee has exhausted their annual leave and sick
leave, they may apply for leave without pay. Approval requires
the Head of Department and the Director, People & Culture.
Leave without pay may not exceed 15 working days in any
calendar year.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT CHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS POLICY (HR-POL-2024-001)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Annual leave increased from 20 to 25 working days
- Carry-forward limit increased from 5 to 10 days, with a
  31 March deadline
- Notice period for leave requests increased from 7 to 10 days
- Manager response time reduced from 3 to 2 working days
- Added compassionate leave, sick leave and parental leave
  (previously in separate, informal guidelines)
- Added December holiday period rules
- Added leave cash-out on termination
- Leave without pay limit increased from 10 to 15 days
