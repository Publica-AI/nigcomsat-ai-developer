1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy explains how NovaNet Technologies Ltd collects,
stores, processes and protects the personal data of its
employees, in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act
(NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)
regulations.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
------------------------------------------------------------
The company collects and maintains the following categories of
employee personal data:

(a) Identity data: full name, date of birth, state of origin,
    passport photograph, National Identification Number (NIN).

(b) Contact data: personal email, phone number, residential
    address, next-of-kin details.

(c) Employment data: job title, department, grade level, salary,
    bank account details, tax identification number (TIN),
    pension fund administrator (PFA) and RSA PIN.

(d) Health data: pre-employment medical report, HMO enrolment
    records, disability status (if voluntarily disclosed).

(e) Performance data: appraisal records, disciplinary records,
    training records.

(f) IT usage data: login timestamps, VPN connection logs,
    email metadata (sender, recipient, timestamp — not content).

------------------------------------------------------------
3. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
------------------------------------------------------------
The company processes employee data on three lawful bases under
the NDPA:

(a) Contract — data necessary to fulfil the employment contract
    (salary payments, pension contributions, HMO enrolment).

(b) Legal obligation — data required by Nigerian tax law, pension
    law, and labour regulations.

(c) Legitimate interest — data used for security monitoring,
    fraud prevention, and workforce planning, where the
    company's interest does not override the employee's rights.

The company does not rely on consent as the primary basis for
processing employment data, because the power imbalance in an
employment relationship makes consent unreliable. However,
consent is sought for optional processing such as publishing
an employee's photograph on the company website.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATA RETENTION
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Active employees: data is retained for the duration of
    employment.

(b) Former employees: employment records are retained for 6
    years after the date of exit, in line with the statute of
    limitations for employment claims. Pension and tax records
    are retained as required by the relevant regulators.

(c) Unsuccessful applicants: recruitment data is retained for
    12 months after the recruitment decision and then deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. EMPLOYEE RIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Under the NDPA, employees have the right to:

(a) Access a copy of their personal data held by the company.
(b) Request correction of inaccurate data.
(c) Request deletion of data that is no longer necessary (subject
    to legal retention obligations).
(d) Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
(e) Receive their data in a portable format (CSV or PDF).

Requests should be submitted to the Data Protection Officer at
dpo@novanet.com.ng. The company will respond within 30 days.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Employee data is stored in the HR Information System (HRIS),
    which is hosted on encrypted servers in Lagos and backed up
    daily to a secondary site in Abuja.

(b) Access to the HRIS is restricted by role: HR staff see
    records for their assigned departments; line managers see
    only their direct reports; the Finance team sees only
    payroll-relevant fields.

(c) Paper records (original certificates held during employment)
    are stored in a locked cabinet in the HR office and returned
    to the employee on exit.

(d) Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data
    Protection Officer within 24 hours. The DPO will assess
    whether the NDPC and affected employees must be notified.
