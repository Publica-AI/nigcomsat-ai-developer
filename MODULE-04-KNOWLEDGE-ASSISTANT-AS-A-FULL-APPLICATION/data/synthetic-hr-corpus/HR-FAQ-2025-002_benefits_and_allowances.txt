Q1: What health insurance does the company provide?
A1: All confirmed employees and up to 3 dependants (spouse
    and children under 18) are enrolled in the company HMO
    plan with HealthPlus Nigeria. Coverage includes
    outpatient care, inpatient care, dental, optical, and
    maternity. The company pays the full premium.

Q2: Can I add more than 3 dependants to my HMO?
A2: Yes, at your own cost. Contact HR Operations for the
    additional dependant premium rates.

Q3: What is the 13th month pay?
A3: A discretionary bonus equivalent to one month's basic
    salary, paid in December. It is subject to company
    performance and is not guaranteed. Employees who join
    after 1 July receive a pro-rated amount. Employees with
    a performance rating of 1 (Unsatisfactory) are not
    eligible.

Q4: How does the pension scheme work?
A4: NovaNet contributes 10% of your basic salary to your
    Retirement Savings Account (RSA), and 8% is deducted from
    your salary, for a total of 18%. You choose your Pension
    Fund Administrator (PFA) during onboarding.

Q5: What is the remote work allowance?
A5: Employees on an approved hybrid or fully remote arrangement
    receive NGN 35,000 per month (increased from NGN 25,000 in
    March 2026 — see circular HR-CIR-2026-003). This covers
    electricity and internet costs. It is taxable.

Q6: Can I get a monitor or chair for my home office?
A6: Yes. Submit a request through the IT portal. Items up to
    NGN 120,000 are approved by the IT Manager. Items above
    that amount need Head of Department approval.

Q7: Does the company pay for co-working spaces?
A7: Only for fully remote employees based outside Lagos. The
    company reimburses up to NGN 40,000 per month with Head
    of Department pre-approval and original receipts. See
    circular HR-CIR-2026-003.

Q8: How much is the travel per diem?
A8: Domestic travel: NGN 15,000 per day. International
    travel: USD 75 per day. The per diem covers meals and
    incidentals and does not require receipts. See
    HR-POL-2025-005.

Q9: What is the accommodation limit for business travel?
A9: It depends on your grade level:
    - Grade 1–4: NGN 35,000 per night
    - Grade 5–7: NGN 55,000 per night
    - Grade 8+: NGN 85,000 per night
    International travel: actual cost up to USD 200 per night
    (exceptions for high-cost cities require Director, Finance
    approval).

Q10: Am I eligible for an annual bonus?
A10: The annual bonus is discretionary and linked to your
     performance rating:
     - Rating 5: up to 20% of annual base salary
     - Rating 4: up to 15%
     - Rating 3: up to 10%
     - Rating 2 or 1: not eligible
     See the Performance Review Procedure (HR-SOP-2025-002).

Q11: When is salary paid?
A11: Salary is credited to your bank account on the 25th of
     each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public
     holiday, payment is made on the preceding working day.

Q12: How do I get a reference letter after leaving?
A12: Contact HR Operations. The company provides factual
     references only: job title, dates of employment, and
     last grade level. Performance details are not included.
     See the Exit Procedure (HR-SOP-2025-003).
