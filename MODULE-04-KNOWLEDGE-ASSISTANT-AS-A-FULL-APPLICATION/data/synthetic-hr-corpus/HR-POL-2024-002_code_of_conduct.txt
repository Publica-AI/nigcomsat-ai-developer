1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes the standards of behaviour expected of
all employees and the procedure the company follows when those
standards are not met.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. STANDARDS OF CONDUCT
------------------------------------------------------------
All employees are expected to:

(a) Carry out their duties with professionalism, honesty and
    respect for colleagues, clients and partners.

(b) Protect company property, intellectual property and
    confidential information. Company-issued devices must not
    be used for personal commercial activity.

(c) Comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
    when handling personal data of customers, employees or
    third parties.

(d) Report any conflict of interest — including secondary
    employment, ownership stakes in suppliers, or family
    relationships with vendors — to their line manager and
    the HR department within 7 days of becoming aware of it.

(e) Not engage in harassment, bullying, discrimination or
    victimisation of any kind. NovaNet operates a zero-
    tolerance policy on workplace harassment.

(f) Refrain from consuming alcohol or controlled substances
    during working hours or on company premises.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. CATEGORIES OF MISCONDUCT
------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Minor misconduct
    - Persistent lateness without explanation
    - Failure to follow dress code (business casual, Monday
      to Thursday; casual Friday)
    - Unauthorised personal use of company resources

3.2 Serious misconduct
    - Insubordination or refusal to carry out reasonable
      instructions
    - Negligence resulting in loss or damage to company
      property valued above NGN 500,000
    - Breach of confidentiality or data protection obligations
    - Unauthorised absence for 3 or more consecutive days

3.3 Gross misconduct (may result in summary dismissal)
    - Fraud, theft or misappropriation of company funds
    - Physical violence or threats of violence
    - Sexual harassment
    - Deliberate destruction of company property
    - Being under the influence of alcohol or controlled
      substances during working hours

------------------------------------------------------------
4. DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 — Verbal warning
    The line manager addresses the issue informally with the
    employee and records the conversation.

Step 2 — First written warning
    Issued by the line manager with a copy to HR. The employee
    has 5 working days to respond in writing. The warning
    remains on file for 6 months.

Step 3 — Final written warning
    Issued by the Head of Department. The employee may bring a
    colleague or union representative to a hearing. The warning
    remains on file for 12 months.

Step 4 — Dismissal or other sanction
    Decided by a panel comprising the Head of Department, the
    Director of People & Culture, and a member of the Legal
    team. The employee is given 10 working days' notice of the
    hearing and may present evidence. The panel may impose
    dismissal, suspension without pay (up to 10 days), demotion,
    or transfer.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. APPEALS
------------------------------------------------------------
An employee may appeal any disciplinary decision in writing to
the Managing Director within 10 working days of receiving the
decision. The appeal is heard by a panel that does not include
the original decision-makers. The appeal decision is final.
