------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines the terms under which employees may work
remotely, the equipment and support provided, and the obligations
that apply while working outside the office.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. ELIGIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Employees who have completed their probation period (6 months)
    and whose role does not require daily physical presence may
    apply for remote work.

(b) Roles excluded from remote work: facilities staff, front-desk
    and reception, hardware engineers who maintain on-site
    equipment, and security personnel.

(c) The line manager and Head of Department must both approve a
    remote work arrangement. HR records the arrangement.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. REMOTE WORK MODELS
------------------------------------------------------------
NovaNet offers two models:

(a) Hybrid — the employee works from the office 3 days per week
    (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are mandatory in-office days)
    and remotely on Monday and Friday.

(b) Fully remote — the employee works from a location outside
    Lagos. Fully remote arrangements are approved on a case-by-
    case basis and require a minimum of one in-office week per
    quarter.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCES
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The IT department provides a company laptop, a VPN licence,
    and access to the company collaboration tools (Microsoft 365
    and Slack).

(b) Employees on an approved remote work arrangement receive a
    monthly Remote Work Allowance of NGN 25,000 to cover
    electricity and internet costs. This allowance is taxable.

(c) Employees who need an ergonomic chair or monitor may request
    one through the IT portal. Requests up to NGN 80,000 are
    approved by the IT Manager; requests above that amount require
    Head of Department approval.

(d) The company does not reimburse co-working space fees unless
    pre-approved by the Head of Department.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Core hours are 09:00 to 16:00 WAT. Employees must be
    reachable on Slack and email during core hours.

(b) The employee's total daily working hours remain 8 hours,
    in line with the standard employment contract.

(c) Remote work does not change overtime eligibility. Overtime
    must be pre-approved by the line manager.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) All company work must be done on the company-issued laptop.
    Employees must not store company data on personal devices.

(b) The VPN must be connected when accessing internal systems.

(c) Employees working from public locations (cafés, co-working
    spaces) must use a privacy screen and must not join
    confidential meetings where conversations can be overheard.

(d) Breaches of data protection obligations while working
    remotely are treated under the Code of Conduct and
    Disciplinary Procedure (HR-POL-2024-002).

------------------------------------------------------------
7. TERMINATION OF REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
Either the employee or the company may end a remote work
arrangement with 2 weeks' written notice. The company may end
an arrangement immediately if the employee's performance falls
below expectations or if a security incident occurs.
