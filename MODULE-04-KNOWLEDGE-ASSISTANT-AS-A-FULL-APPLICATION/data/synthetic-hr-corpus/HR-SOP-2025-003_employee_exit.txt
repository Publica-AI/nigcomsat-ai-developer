Department:             HR / IT / Finance
Date issued:            2025-04-01
Approved by:            Director, People & Culture
Status:                 CURRENT

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This procedure covers the steps from the point an employee
resigns or is terminated to their final exit from the company.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. NOTICE PERIOD
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The standard notice period is:
    - Grade Level 1–4: 1 month
    - Grade Level 5–7: 2 months
    - Grade Level 8 and above: 3 months

(b) The company may waive the notice period and pay salary in
    lieu of notice at its discretion.

(c) An employee who leaves without serving the required notice
    forfeits salary in lieu of the unserved period.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. RESIGNATION PROCESS
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The employee submits a resignation letter to their line
    manager and copies HR at hr-ops@novanet.com.ng.

(b) The line manager acknowledges receipt and confirms the
    last working day based on the notice period.

(c) HR schedules an exit interview within the final week of
    employment. Participation is voluntary but encouraged.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) During the notice period, the departing employee must:
    - Document all ongoing projects, open tasks and key contacts
    - Hand over all files, credentials and access to shared
      resources to the designated successor or line manager
    - Complete any outstanding deliverables or agree a handover
      plan with the line manager

(b) The line manager signs off on the knowledge transfer
    checklist in the HRIS.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. IT OFFBOARDING
------------------------------------------------------------
The IT department completes the following on the employee's
last working day:

(a) Revoke access to all company systems: Microsoft 365, Slack,
    VPN, HRIS, department-specific tools.
(b) Collect the company laptop, charger, and any peripherals.
(c) Wipe company data from any personal devices that were
    enrolled in the MDM (Mobile Device Management) system.
(d) Forward the employee's email to their line manager for 30
    days, then deactivate the account.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. FINANCE — FINAL SETTLEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The Finance team calculates the final settlement, which
    includes:
    - Salary up to the last working day
    - Pro-rated 13th month pay (if applicable)
    - Payment for unused annual leave days (at the daily rate)
    - Any outstanding expense reimbursements
    - Deductions: outstanding salary advances, equipment
      not returned, unserved notice period

(b) The final settlement is paid within 30 days of the last
    working day.

(c) The employee's pension contributions are not paid out by
    the company; the employee contacts their PFA directly.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. CLEARANCE
------------------------------------------------------------
The employee must complete the clearance form, signed by:
- Line manager (knowledge transfer complete)
- IT (all equipment returned)
- Finance (no outstanding advances)
- Facilities (access card returned)
- HR (exit interview completed or declined)

The clearance form must be fully signed before the final
settlement is processed.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. REFERENCE LETTERS
------------------------------------------------------------
Former employees may request a reference letter from HR. The
company provides factual references only: job title, dates of
employment, and last grade level. Performance assessments are
not shared in reference letters.
